Confounders include 10-year yields, term premia, MBS spreads, inflation expectations, and Fed communication. Further analysis would use event studies around FOMC decisions and regressions of mortgage-rate changes on fed funds changes while controlling for 10-year yields and MBS spreads.

That does not show stocks rise with inflation; if anything, it shows no stable short-run relationship. Further analysis would examine real returns against inflation surprises, use longer horizons and subperiods, and control for growth and rate changes.
