These data have important limits for judging household well-being. They are mostly aggregate and national; they do not show median or lower-income households, debt service, wealth, rents, or regional variation. Real PCE is total spending, not a welfare measure, and the saving rate can be revised. Sentiment is subjective. CPI is an average basket, not any household’s cost of living. October 2025 CPI and unemployment are missing, so some month-to-month and 12-month comparisons cannot be made.
