## What Seshat is

**Seshat** (publication brand; repository name remains FinStory, D-012) is an
independent financial storytelling publication, named for the ancient
Egyptian goddess of writing, mathematics and measurement. It makes
economics, markets and wealth understandable and useful to ordinary readers
while offering real analytical depth to more sophisticated investors.

Guiding principle: **the presentation is the product; analytics is the
infrastructure.** Seshat is a premium digital magazine, not a terminal,
trading app, robo-advisor or chatbot. Human editorial judgment drives the
product; AI works behind the scenes (later phases).

## Story model — five layers

| Layer | Purpose |
|-------|---------|
| 1. Headline | The development and why it matters, understandable in seconds ("The story in 30 seconds") |
| 2. Narrative | Human explanation — the heart of the product |
| 3. Macro | 3–4 indicators and 1–2 charts, each answering one question with an explicit takeaway |
| 4. Analytics | Transmission mechanisms and implications; every claim tagged *In the data* / *Established link* / *Our view* |
| 5. Outlook | Conditional scenarios: conditions, why they matter, assets affected, signposts. No probabilities |

Complexity is disclosed progressively: chapter navigation, "How it's
measured" and "Show the data" disclosures, methodology at the end.

## Phase 2A scope (delivered)

- Editorial homepage (lead story, supporting previews, sections).
- One complete demonstration story: *The Bond Comeback* on real FRED data.
- Supporting stories shown as clearly marked **In preparation** previews.
- Section pages, About / standards page.

Explicitly excluded in 2A: admin auth, CMS, AI research or Arli AI calls,
automated web search, live data ingestion, AI-generated articles,
subscriptions, personalization, portfolios, trading, forecasting engines.

## Editorial & data standards (binding)

- Genuine data only, with source, dates and calculations disclosed; missing
  values are never estimated silently (D-018).
- No AI-generated imagery anywhere in the public product (D-020).
- Scenarios are illustrative editorial analysis, not model forecasts; no
  invented probabilities or attribution percentages (D-019).
- General information and education, not investment advice.
